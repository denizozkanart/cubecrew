Cube Crew: Sort, Stack, Build! — Support

Cube Crew is a puzzle game by Kyro Games. Send the right crates, and the trucks and the tower crane raise a voxel build from the ground up.

Contact

Questions, bug reports or ideas: dozkanart@gmail.com. We usually answer within a few days.

Common questions

I ran out of space. What can I do? Use the Bulldozer to clear the bays, the Crane to put a parked crate back, or Shuffle to restack the pallets. When the bays are full you can also clear them with coins and keep building.

Is my progress saved? Yes, on your device. Deleting the app removes it.

How do I get more coins? Finish levels and take the daily gift in the Shop.
