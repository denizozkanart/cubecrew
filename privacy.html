Cube Crew: Sort, Stack, Build! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Cube Crew is made by Kyro Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

The game has no accounts of its own, and we do not ask for your name, e-mail or any other personal information. Your progress (level, coins, boosters and settings) is saved on your device.

Game Center

On iOS the game can sign in to Apple's Game Center, if you use it, to post the number of levels you have built to the "Levels Built" leaderboard. Apple runs Game Center under its own privacy policy; we receive only your score and your Game Center nickname as shown on the leaderboard.

To understand how the game is played and to fix problems, Cube Crew uses Google Firebase:

  • Firebase Analytics records anonymous game events, such as starting, finishing or failing a level, using a booster or claiming the daily gift, together with basic technical information (device model, operating system version, app version, country derived from the network). These events are tied to a random app-instance identifier, not to you.
  • Firebase Crashlytics sends a report when the game crashes (the state of the app and device at the time of the crash) so we can fix the error.

Firebase does not collect the device's advertising identifier. Google processes this data on our behalf under its own terms (Firebase privacy). We do not sell your data.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game books a few reminders on your device (for example when your daily gift is ready). They are scheduled locally; nothing is sent to a server. You can turn them off in the game's settings or in your device's Settings.

Advertising

Cube Crew shows ads: a short video you choose to watch for a reward, and now and then an ad between levels (not after you buy No Ads). The ads come through Unity LevelPlay (ironSource and its ad partners). To show and measure ads they receive information such as the device model and operating system, the app, the IP address (from which an approximate country or region is derived) and how you interact with the ads.

On iOS the game asks once, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier (IDFA) to be more relevant to you. If you say no, the identifier is not shared and you still see ads, just less personalised ones. You can change the answer at any time in your iPhone's Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Unity's privacy policy: unity.com/legal.

In-app purchases

Coin packs, bundles and No Ads are sold through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we do not receive your payment details. The game keeps a record of the purchases it has delivered on your device.

Children

Cube Crew is meant for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game does not ask for a name, e-mail or any other contact details.
