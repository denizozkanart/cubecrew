Cube Crew: Sort, Stack, Build! — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 October 2026

Cube Crew is made by Kyro Games ("we"). This policy explains what information the game uses.

What we collect

The current version of Cube Crew shows no ads and does not collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts and no sign-in. Your progress (level, coins, boosters and settings) is saved only on your device.

Advertising and analytics

If a future version shows ads or uses analytics, it will use third-party services that may collect your device's advertising identifier and basic usage data to show ads and measure how the game is played. On iOS you will be asked for permission first (App Tracking Transparency), and you can say no or change it at any time in Settings. This policy will be updated before such a version is released.

In-app purchases

Purchases are handled by Apple. We do not receive your payment details.

Children

Cube Crew is suitable for all ages and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
